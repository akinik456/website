Privacy Policy – LynraFleet

LynraFleet is a vehicle tracking and fleet management application designed to help businesses monitor vehicles and stay connected with their drivers.

Information We Collect

LynraFleet stores information required for the service to function, including driver names, fleet information, vehicle information, device identifiers, and location data shared between authorized fleet members.

Location Data

Location data is used only to provide vehicle tracking, fleet management, and safety features. Location information is shared only with authorized members of the same fleet.

Notifications

The app may use push notifications to deliver vehicle alerts, safety notifications, and service-related notifications.

Data Storage

Data may be stored using secure cloud services required for application functionality, including Firebase services provided by Google.

Third-Party Services

LynraFleet uses Google Firebase services for application functionality, notifications, and secure data synchronization.

Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect user information and restrict access to authorized fleet members.

Data Deletion

Users may request the deletion of their account and associated personal data at any time.

To request deletion, please send an email to lynra.dev@gmail.com with sufficient information to identify your account.

After verifying the request, we will permanently delete the user's account and associated personal data from our systems within a reasonable period, unless we are required to retain certain information by applicable law.

Uninstalling the application does not automatically delete your account or stored data.
