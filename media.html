About LynraFamily

LynraFamily is a privacy-focused location sharing app that helps couples, families, friends and loved ones stay connected through live location sharing and smart safety features.

Unlike many alternatives, LynraFamily has no ads, no subscriptions and uses a simple pay once, use forever model.
